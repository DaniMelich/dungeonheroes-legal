Privacy Policy — Dungeon Heroes

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SunForge (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you play Dungeon Heroes (the “Game”) on Android.

1. Who we are

The Game is developed and published by SunForge.

  • Developer / data controller: SunForge
  • Contact: sunforgesupport@gmail.com

If you have any question about this policy or your data, email us at the address above.

2. Summary (the short version)

  • We do not ask you for your name, email, or any account sign-up to play.
  • You are identified by an anonymous player ID generated on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your Play Games player ID is linked to it so your progress survives a reinstall or a new device. Signing in is optional.
  • We use third-party services to save your progress to the cloud, run leaderboards, send notifications, understand how the Game is used (analytics), show advertisements, and process in-app purchases.
  • Ads and analytics involve an advertising identifier and are provided by third parties (see Section 5).
  • You can request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 8).

3. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal contact details. Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. The information collected while you play falls into the following categories:

a. Identifiers

  • An anonymous player ID (a pseudonymous identifier created by Unity Gaming Services to recognise your save and leaderboard entries across sessions).
  • If you sign in with Google Play Games: your Google Play Games player ID, which Unity Gaming Services links to your anonymous player ID so that your cloud save can be restored after a reinstall or on a new device. We do not receive your Google account email address or password.
  • A device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID), used for advertising and measurement.
  • A push notification token (if notifications are enabled), used to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Approximate technical identifiers such as IP address, processed by our service providers to operate their services. The IP address may be used to estimate your approximate location (such as your country); we do not collect precise location.

b. Device and technical information

  • Device model, operating-system version, language/region, app version, and similar technical attributes.

c. Gameplay and usage data

  • In-game progress and profile data (levels, unlocks, run/meta progression) that make up your cloud save.
  • Leaderboard scores and rankings.
  • Analytics events describing how the Game is used (for example, session starts, screens viewed, and gameplay milestones), used to fix problems and improve the Game.

d. Advertising data

  • Information related to the ads shown to you (such as ad views, clicks, and the advertising identifier), used by our advertising partners to deliver and measure ads.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, your advertising consent choices, which are stored on your device in the standard IAB TCF format and read by our advertising partners.

e. Purchase information

  • If you make an in-app purchase, payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We receive the product you bought, the purchase status and a transaction identifier, and keep a one-way fingerprint of that identifier and the items you own in your save, so that purchases are delivered exactly once and can be restored.
  • Purchase events (such as the product, price and currency) are also recorded by Unity Analytics, and our advertising partners may receive information about in-app purchases to measure and improve advertising.

We do not knowingly collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment card details through the Game.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Save and restore your game progress across sessions and devices (cloud save);
  • Operate leaderboards and show rankings;
  • Send notifications you have enabled;
  • Link your progress to your Google Play Games account, if you sign in;
  • Process, deliver, and restore in-app purchases;
  • Ask for and record your advertising consent choices where the law requires it;
  • Understand and improve gameplay, stability, and performance (analytics);
  • Display and measure advertising;
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, and abuse;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

6. Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK users)

Where the EU/UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to run the Game, save your progress, operate leaderboards you choose to use, link your progress to your Google Play Games account if you sign in, and deliver and restore your purchases.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the Game secure, prevent abuse, and improve it through analytics, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — for personalised advertising and, where required, analytics. Where consent is required, we ask for it and you may withdraw it at any time through the in-game options or your device settings.

7. Data retention

  • Cloud save, leaderboard, and analytics data are retained for as long as the associated anonymous player ID remains active, and thereafter for the periods set by our service providers, unless you request deletion earlier.
  • Google Play Games link and purchase records (the transaction fingerprint and the items you own) are kept with your save for as long as it exists, unless you request deletion earlier. Google keeps its own order records under its policy.
  • Advertising data is retained according to each advertising partner's policy.
  • We delete or anonymise data we no longer need.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your data, to object to processing, and to data portability.

  • Reset your advertising ID / opt out of personalised ads: use your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Disable notifications: turn them off in your Android app settings.
  • Delete your data: because we do not collect contact details, deletion is handled via the process described in our Data Deletion Policy — or email sunforgesupport@gmail.com with your request.

To help us locate your anonymous data, the Game shows your player ID on the Settings screen; include it in your request where possible.

We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the Game shows advertising and uses analytics, it is intended for a general audience of teens and adults. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process and store information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) are applied by those providers.

11. Region-specific disclosures

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): see Sections 6 and 8 for legal bases and your rights. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the “sale”/“sharing” of personal information. Sharing of the advertising identifier for personalised ads may constitute “sharing”; opt out via your device's ad settings and the in-game options.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated within the Game or through the store listing.
