Data Deletion Policy — Dungeon Heroes

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This page explains what data Dungeon Heroes, published by SunForge, keeps about you, where it is kept, and how to have it deleted. It is provided to satisfy Google Play's data-deletion requirements. For the full picture of what we collect and why, see the Privacy Policy.

Where your data is stored

Dungeon Heroes does not ask for your name, email address or a password. You are identified by an anonymous player ID created by Unity Gaming Services. The Game shows it on the Settings screen.

On your device

  • Your game progress and settings, your run history, the items you own and your purchase records (see below), stored in the Game's app storage.

With Unity Gaming Services, under your anonymous player ID

  • Cloud save: a copy of your game progress, including your purchase records.
  • Google Play Games link: if you signed in with Google Play Games, your Play Games player ID is linked to your anonymous player ID. This is what lets you recover your progress after a reinstall or on another device.
  • Leaderboard entries, if the Game has submitted any.
  • Analytics events: pseudonymous usage data and purchase events (such as the product, price and currency), used to improve the Game.

Purchase records. If you buy something in the Game, payment is handled by Google Play Billing. We keep a one-way fingerprint of each transaction identifier and the items you own, in your save on the device and in your cloud save, so that each purchase is delivered exactly once and can be restored.

With Google. Your payment details and order history are kept by Google in your Google Play account, and your Play Games profile belongs to your Google account. We do not hold or control this data. Google's own settings and the Google Privacy Policy govern it.

With our advertising partners. Unity Ads and ironSource (through Unity LevelPlay) process data tied to your device's advertising ID, not to your anonymous player ID. We cannot look this data up or delete it by your player ID. Each partner keeps it under its own policy (see the Privacy Policy, section 5).

Uninstalling and reinstalling

Uninstalling the Game deletes the data on your device only. It does not delete anything held by Unity Gaming Services.

  • If you have not signed in with Google Play Games, a reinstall creates a new anonymous player ID. Your old cloud save stays on the server, but the Game can no longer reach it. To have it deleted, note your player ID from the Settings screen before you uninstall and include it in your request.
  • If you have signed in with Google Play Games, reinstalling the Game (or installing it on another device) signs you back in to the same player and restores your cloud save. Reinstalling does not delete your cloud save. Only a deletion request does.

How to request deletion

Send an email to sunforgesupport@gmail.com with the subject line “Data deletion request — Dungeon Heroes”.

Include your anonymous player ID, which is shown on the Game's Settings screen. If you signed in with Google Play Games, you can still read it there after a reinstall. If you uninstalled without signing in and did not note your player ID, we may be unable to identify your old cloud save.

We will:

  1. Acknowledge your request.
  2. Delete the data held under your player ID by Unity Gaming Services: your cloud save (including your purchase records), your Google Play Games link, your leaderboard entries and your analytics data. We will also ask our processors to do the same.
  3. Confirm that it is done.

We aim to complete deletion within 30 days, or sooner where the law requires.

Deletion cannot be undone. Your progress and any consumable items are lost. Permanent purchases and active subscriptions remain on your Google Play account and can be restored from Google Play if you play again.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted on request: your cloud save and purchase records, your Google Play Games link, your leaderboard entries, and analytics data tied to your player ID.
  • Kept:
    • Google's order records and your Play Games profile, which Google keeps under its own policy.
    • Data held by our advertising partners under your advertising ID, kept under each partner's policy.
    • Aggregated or anonymised statistics that can no longer be linked to you.
    • Records we are legally required to keep.

Things you can do yourself

  • Delete the data on your device: uninstall the Game, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Dungeon Heroes → Storage.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Manage your Play Games profile: use your Google account's Play Games settings.
