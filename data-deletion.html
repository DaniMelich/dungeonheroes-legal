Data Deletion Policy — Dungeon Heroes

Last updated: 24 July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of data associated with your use of Dungeon Heroes, published by SunForge. It is provided to satisfy Google Play's account/data-deletion requirements.

What data is associated with you

Dungeon Heroes does not require an account, name, or email to play. You are identified only by an anonymous player ID generated on your device by Unity Gaming Services. The data linked to that ID may include:

  • Cloud save — your in-game progress and profile (levels, unlocks, run/meta progression).
  • Leaderboard entries — your scores and rankings.
  • Analytics events — pseudonymous usage data used to improve the Game.
  • Advertising identifiers — processed by advertising partners (see the Privacy Policy).

How to request deletion

Send an email to dungeonheroessupport@gmail.com with the subject line “Data deletion request — Dungeon Heroes”.

To help us locate and delete your data, please include your anonymous player ID where possible. You can find it on the Settings screen inside the Game.

We will:

  1. Acknowledge your request;
  2. Delete the cloud save, leaderboard, and analytics data associated with your player ID, and instruct our processors to do the same;
  3. Confirm completion.

We aim to complete deletion within 30 days, or sooner where required by law.

Deleting data yourself

  • Uninstalling the Game removes the local copy of your data from your device. Note that the anonymous session token is stored in app data and does not survive an uninstall, so a reinstall starts a fresh anonymous identity.
  • Reset advertising ID / opt out of personalised ads: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

What is deleted vs. retained

  • Deleted on request: cloud save, leaderboard entries, and analytics data tied to your anonymous player ID.
  • May be retained: aggregated or anonymised statistics that can no longer be linked to you, and records we are legally required to keep.
